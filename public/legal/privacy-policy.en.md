## Introduction

As part of its operation, Redlink collects and processes personal data.

## Personal data collected

- **Identity**: last name, first name
- **Contact details**: email address, phone number, postal address
- **Geolocation**: GPS coordinates of the address provided, used to calculate the distance between a donor animal and a clinic
- **Animal-related data**: species, breed, blood group, donation history, availability

## Use of data

This data is used exclusively to match veterinary clinics with donor animal owners.

## Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your data, exercisable directly from your personal space (account deletion) or by contacting us via the Support page.
